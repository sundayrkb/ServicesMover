Terms of Service

Last updated: September 2025

1. Agreement to Terms

By requesting a quote, booking a move, or using the services of Bhartiya Packers & Movers ("BPM", "we", "us", "our"), you ("Customer", "you") agree to these Terms of Service. If you do not agree, do not use our services.

2. Services Provided

BPM provides packing, loading, transportation, unloading, unpacking, and rearrangement services for household goods, office equipment, vehicles, and other items as agreed in writing. Services may include:

  • Home relocation (local and interstate)
  • Office and commercial moving
  • Vehicle transport (cars, bikes)
  • Storage solutions (short and long-term)
  • Premium packing and fragile-only packing
  • Insurance coverage options

Specific services, timelines, and pricing are detailed in your written quote/agreement.

3. Quotes and Booking

  • Quotes are valid for 30 days from issuance.
  • Final pricing may vary based on actual inventory, access conditions, and additional services requested on move day.
  • Booking is confirmed upon receipt of advance payment (typically 10-25% of estimated total).
  • BPM reserves the right to reschedule due to force majeure (weather, strikes, road closures) with reasonable notice.

4. Customer Responsibilities

The Customer agrees to:

  • Provide accurate inventory and access information (floor, lift, parking restrictions)
  • Obtain necessary permissions from housing societies, building management, local authorities
  • Segregate and personally transport valuables (jewelry, cash, documents, medications)
  • Declare any hazardous, flammable, or prohibited items (none will be transported)
  • Be present or authorize a representative at both pickup and delivery
  • Pay the balance amount upon delivery as per agreed terms

5. Packing and Handling

  • BPM uses industry-standard packing materials (boxes, bubble wrap, packing paper, tape, blankets).
  • Fragile items receive extra protection; "fragile-only" packing available on request.
  • Furniture is dismantled and reassembled where feasible (beds, tables, wardrobes).
  • Appliances must be disconnected, defrosted, and dry before packing.

6. Transit and Delivery

  • GPS tracking provided for interstate moves via WhatsApp link.
  • Estimated delivery windows are provided; delays due to traffic, weather, or road conditions are not liability events.
  • Delivery is to the ground floor/designated area unless lift/suitable access is available and agreed.
  • Unpacking and placement included; debris removal included unless otherwise agreed.

7. Insurance and Liability

  • Basic Transit Insurance: Included at no extra cost, covering loss/damage during transit up to declared value (terms apply).
  • Enhanced Coverage: Available up to ₹50 lakhs for additional premium.
  • Exclusions: Pre-existing damage, inherent vice, inadequate customer packing, force majeure, consequential loss.
  • Claims: Must be reported within 24 hours of delivery with photographic evidence. Survey arranged within 48 hours.
  • Maximum Liability: Limited to the insured declared value or actual repair/replacement cost, whichever is lower.

8. Cancellation and Rescheduling

  • Cancellation > 7 days before move: Full refund of advance.
  • Cancellation 3-7 days before: 50% of advance forfeited.
  • Cancellation < 3 days before: Advance forfeited.
  • Rescheduling > 48 hours: No charge (subject to availability).
  • Rescheduling < 48 hours: ₹2,000 fee.

9. Payment Terms

  • Advance payment confirms booking (UPI, card, bank transfer, cash).
  • Balance due on delivery before unloading commences.
  • Late payment attracts 18% per annum interest.
  • GST applicable as per current rates (included in quote).

10. Prohibited Items

BPM will not transport: explosives, flammables, corrosives, weapons, live animals, plants, perishable food, illegal substances, cash, jewelry, important documents, or items prohibited by law.

11. Force Majeure

BPM is not liable for delays or failure to perform due to events beyond reasonable control: natural disasters, strikes, lockouts, government actions, war, terrorism, pandemics, road closures, or severe weather.

12. Dispute Resolution

  • Governing Law: Laws of India.
  • Jurisdiction: Courts of Hyderabad, Telangana.
  • Mediation encouraged before litigation.

13. Limitation of Liability

Except for gross negligence or willful misconduct, BPM's total liability shall not exceed the total contract value. BPM is not liable for indirect, consequential, or punitive damages.

14. Severability

If any provision is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force.
