Hyderabad-Specific Risks Covered

Our insurance accounts for Hyderabad's unique moving challenges:

  • Monsoon Damage (Jun-Sept): Water ingress during loading/unloading, humidity damage to electronics/artwork, slip-related handling incidents — all covered under comprehensive & premium tiers
  • High-Rise Risks (Gachibowli, Hitech City, Kondapur): Elevator malfunction damage, floor/wall scratches during furniture movement, balcony loading incidents — covered up to policy limit
  • Narrow Lane Access (Old City, Tolichowki, Secunderabad): Hand cart damage, manual handling mishaps, vehicle access scrapes — covered under all tiers
  • Cantonment/Defense Area (Secunderabad): Security delay costs, escort vehicle damage, restricted timing penalties — covered under premium tier
  • ORR/Highway Transit: Road debris, sudden braking, animal strikes on NH-44/NH-65/NH-16 — comprehensive vehicle coverage
  • Summer Heat (Mar-May): Heat damage to electronics, artwork melting/warping, leather cracking — covered with climate-controlled packing endorsement

Hyderabad-Specific Coverage Tiers

  • Basic (₹2L Included): Local Hyderabad moves (intra-city). Covers basic transit liability. Suitable for 1-2 BHK local shifts.
  • Comprehensive (₹10L): Inter-city (Hyderabad to Bangalore/Chennai/Mumbai). Covers packing, loading, transit, unloading, unpacking. Standard for 3-4 BHK moves.
  • Premium (₹50L): High-value moves — Banjara Hills villas, Hitech City office relocations, luxury vehicle transport, artwork/antiques. Dedicated claims manager.
  • Vehicle IDV: Car/bike transport — covers up to Insured Declared Value. Pre/post condition reports mandatory.

Hyderabad-Specific Claims Advantages

  • Local Surveyors: Hyderabad-based surveyors for 24-hr assessment (Banjara Hills, Gachibowli, Secunderabad, Kukatpally)
  • Monsoon Fast-Track: Jun-Sept claims — 24-hr assessment, 48-hr settlement for water/humidity damage
  • High-Rise Specialist: Elevator damage, corridor/wall damage claims — empanelled contractors for immediate repair quotes
  • Cantonment Claims: Secunderabad defense area — coordinated with defense authorities for survey access
  • Art/Heritage Claims: Bidriware, Nizam-era artifacts, Deccan paintings — specialist valuers from Hyderabad (Salar Jung Museum network)

Hyderabad-Specific Exclusions & Clarifications

  • Pre-existing Damage: Documented in pre-move condition report (WhatsApp photos + digital inventory)
  • Inherent Vice: Bidriware natural oxidation, antique wood cracking due to age — not covered unless climate-controlled packing endorsed
  • Monsoon Pre-existing: Pre-existing damp/mold in stored items — not covered unless climate-controlled storage endorsed
  • Cantonment Delays: Defense authority access delays beyond 48h — covered only with premium tier + cantonment endorsement
