Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our services.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2025

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you request a quote, book a move, or contact us:

  • Contact Information: Name, phone number, email address, pickup and delivery addresses
  • Move Details: Inventory list, property type, floor numbers, special requirements
  • Communication Records: WhatsApp chats, call logs, email correspondence
  • Payment Information: Processed securely through our payment partners (we do not store full card details)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide accurate quotes and move planning
  • Coordinate and execute your relocation
  • Communicate updates via WhatsApp, SMS, email, or phone
  • Process payments and issue invoices
  • Comply with legal obligations (insurance, contracts)
  • Improve our services and customer experience

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Service Partners: Verified packing crews, transporters, insurance providers (only what's necessary for your move)
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law or to protect our rights
  • Analytics Providers: Anonymized usage data (Google Analytics, GTM) to improve our website

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, typically:

  • Move records: 7 years (for insurance and legal compliance)
  • Contact inquiries: 2 years
  • Website analytics: 26 months (Google Analytics default)

5. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@bhartiyapackersmovers.com or call +91-9293120124.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information:

  • HTTPS encryption for all website traffic
  • Secure payment processing via PCI-DSS compliant partners
  • Access controls on internal systems
  • Regular security assessments

7. Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies for:

  • Essential cookies: Site functionality, session management
  • Analytics cookies: Google Analytics (GA4) to understand site usage
  • Marketing cookies: Google Tag Manager for conversion tracking

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect site functionality.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party sites (WhatsApp, Google Maps, payment gateways). We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Review their policies before providing information.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected such information, contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Material changes will be communicated via email or prominent website notice.
